POST-OP CARE — VERSION 11 CHANGELOG

Added optional patient Sex information:
- Patient registration: Sex selector appears only when Patient is selected; choices are Male, Female, and Not Specified.
- Doctor Add Patient form: doctor can record the patient's Sex.
- Edit Patient Details: doctor can update the saved Sex value.
- Patient Details & Reports: Sex is displayed in the medical record and included in the follow-up report.
- Existing patient records created in earlier versions are migrated to Not Specified without losing other saved information.

The field is optional and does not block registration or patient creation. This remains a local-storage front-end prototype.
