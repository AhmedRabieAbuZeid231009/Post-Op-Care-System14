Post-Op Care V12 – Patient Sex / Gender Field Visibility Fix

This update makes the optional Sex / Gender field visible in all key patient workflows:
- Patient registration: shown after selecting Patient and Register.
- Patient Dashboard: displays saved Sex / Gender in a Patient Information panel.
- Patient Dashboard: Edit details lets the patient update their own Sex / Gender.
- Doctor Add Patient and Edit Patient Details: includes the same selector.
- Patient Details & Reports and the printable follow-up report: displays the saved value.
- Existing demo patient records safely default to Not Specified; no saved data is erased.

Options: Male, Female, Not Specified. The field is optional.

This remains a front-end demonstration using browser localStorage, not a live clinical system.
