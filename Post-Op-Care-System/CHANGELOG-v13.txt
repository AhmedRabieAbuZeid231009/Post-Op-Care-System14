POST-OP CARE V13 — SEPARATE ROLE WORKSPACES, MESSAGING & PROGRESS CLARITY

1. Role separation
- Patient login opens only the Patient Workspace navigation.
- Doctor login opens only the Doctor Workspace navigation.
- A patient cannot navigate into doctor pages, and a doctor cannot navigate into patient pages.
- The cross-role demo switch is hidden.
- Public registration creates patient accounts only. Doctor accounts are provisioned by an administrator/system owner; doctors sign in with assigned credentials.

2. Patient-doctor communication
- Added a Messages screen to both workspaces.
- Patients can send a message to their assigned doctor.
- Doctors can choose a patient and reply in that patient's conversation.
- Messages are timestamped and saved in browser localStorage for this front-end demonstration.
- This is not real-time communication and does not transmit to another device/browser or a hospital.

3. Recovery percentage explanation
- Clarified that the percentage means follow-up care-plan completion, not biological healing, a probability of recovery, or a prediction of recovery time.
- Added a clinician-facing explanation with example milestone weights: check-ins 25%, medication tracking 25%, wound updates 25%, and follow-up/review 25%.
- These weights are examples only; clinicians should set procedure-specific milestones and weights.
- A normal check-in no longer automatically increases the percentage. Initial sample percentages remain illustrative and must be clinician-reviewed.
- The doctor can update the care-plan completion value in the patient record.
