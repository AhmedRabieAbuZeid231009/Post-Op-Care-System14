POST-OP CARE — VERSION 14

FRONT-END CHANGES
1. Patient registration collects full name, email, password, sex, phone number, surgery/procedure, surgery date, and doctor invitation code.
2. A patient cannot complete registration with an unknown invitation code.
3. Demo doctor invitation code is AHMED123 and is displayed in the Doctor Dashboard.
4. Successful registration creates a patient record linked to the selected doctor by doctor email.
5. Doctor Dashboard, alerts, patient directory, reports, and message patient selector show only patients assigned to the signed-in doctor.
6. Patient message view displays the name of the assigned doctor.
7. Doctor-created patient records are linked to the currently signed-in doctor account.
8. Role-specific navigation remains separate. The workspace switch control is hidden.

DEMO FLOW
1. Sign in as doctor@postopcare.com / 123456.
2. Copy invitation code AHMED123 from Doctor Dashboard.
3. Log out and choose Patient > Register.
4. Complete patient details and enter AHMED123.
5. The patient account is created and opens Patient Dashboard.
6. Log out and sign in as the doctor again in the same browser. The new patient appears in the assigned patient list and patient reports.

LIMITATION
All data remains in browser localStorage for front-end demonstration. Different devices do not synchronize. Cross-device delivery and secure invitation validation are backend responsibilities.
